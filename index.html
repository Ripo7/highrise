How to play

Build it. Ride it.

A wild mountain, all yours. Trace your piste through it point by point, shape the snow around it, then ride it.

  1. Trace Click the snow near the top to put the start, then click further down: the piste follows your points, carved and groomed into the mountain, trees cleared. Drag a point to move it, drag the piste to add a bend. New piste starts another one: begin it on a piste to branch off, end it on one to join it. Tap the snow near the top to put the start, then tap further down: the piste follows your points, carved and groomed into the mountain, trees cleared. Drag a point to move it, drag the piste to add a bend. New piste starts another one: begin it on a piste to branch off, end it on one to join it.
  2. Shape Terrain and Nature: pick a bump, a hollow, a flat, a wall, a rock band, forest or powder and paint it on the 10 m grid. Pieces side by side melt into one shape, and the piste follows them. Terrain and Nature: pick a bump, a hollow, a flat, a wall, a rock band, forest or powder and paint it with your finger. Pieces side by side melt into one shape, and the piste follows them.
  3. Ride Send skier. ← → steer, ↓ brakes, hold Space and release to jump, ↑ ↓ flip and ← → spin in the air. Leave the piste for the powder if you like: reach the finish or the bottom of the mountain. Send skier. Left thumb steers (drag down to brake), right thumb: hold Jump, release to pop. In the air, slide to spin, drag up or down to flip. Leave the piste for the powder if you like.
  4. Look around Drag to orbit (or right-drag while painting), middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. One finger turns the view while tracing (or pick Look), two fingers move and pinch to zoom.
