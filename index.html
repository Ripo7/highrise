Every run is 1.2 km from gate to finish. Start from a blank face or a ready-made line, then sculpt it your way.

How to play

Build the fastest run

Shape the mountain, send the skier, beat your best time. Jumps are fun, but land too hard and you crash.

  1. Sculpt Drag on the snow. Hold still to keep building. Drag one finger on the snow. Hold still to keep building.
  2. Look around Right-drag or Alt-drag to orbit, Shift+right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Two fingers to move along the run and pinch to zoom. Pick Orbit to turn the view.
  3. Send and ride Press Send skier or Space, then ride: arrow keys steer, ↑ tucks, ↓ brakes. Or let go and watch the terrain do the work. Tap Send skier, then ride: hold ◀ ▶ to steer, Tuck to go faster, Brake to slow down. Or let go and watch the terrain do the work.
