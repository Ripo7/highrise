How to play

Build the fastest run

Shape the mountain, send the skier, beat your best time. Jumps are fun, but land too hard and you crash.

  1. Sculpt Drag on the snow. Hold still to keep building. Drag one finger on the snow. Hold still to keep building.
  2. Look around Right-drag or Alt-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Two fingers to orbit and pinch to zoom.
  3. Send the skier Press Send skier or Space. The dashed blue line is your last run. Tap Send skier. The dashed blue line is your last run.
