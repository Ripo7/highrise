How to play

Draw it. Build it. Ride it.

Draw your own descent, shape it into a playground, then ride it: pop off kickers, spin and flip, land clean and chain tricks into combos.

  1. Draw Trace the run seen from the side, as long and as steep as you like (up to 4 km). Redraw it any time with Profile: your sculpting moves with it.
  2. Build Drag on the snow to raise or dig. Use Build (B) to drag out kickers, tables, berms and rollers. Drag on the snow to raise or dig. Use Build to drag out kickers, tables, berms and rollers.
  3. Look around Right-drag or Alt-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, Relief (T) to see bumps and hollows in colour. Two fingers to move and pinch to zoom; pick Orbit to turn the view. Relief shows bumps and hollows in colour.
  4. Ride ← → steer, ↓ brakes. Hold Space to crouch, release to jump: time it at a kicker's lip. In the air, ← → spin and ↑ ↓ flip. Hold Space again to soak up big landings. Stay above 100 km/h for a speed bonus. Left thumb: slide to steer, drag down to brake. Right thumb: hold Jump to crouch, release to pop at a kicker's lip. In the air, slide to spin and drag up or down to flip. Hold Jump again to soak up big landings. Stay above 100 km/h for a speed bonus.
